Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 18, 2026

Your privacy is absolute. AIGymBro is engineered from the ground up on one core principle: **no video data ever leaves your device**.

1. Camera & Video Data Processing

To analyze your posture and count reps in real-time, AIGymBro requires access to your device's camera.

  • Pose analysis runs entirely in memory (RAM) using the **MoveNet** (TensorFlow.js) model.
  • The camera feed is processed locally on your phone's GPU (WebGL) and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded to any server.
  • As soon as you finish your set or close the app, all processing terminates immediately.

2. Health Data Sinc (Apple HealthKit)

AIGymBro integrates with Apple HealthKit on iOS to read and display your daily step counts on the dashboard.

  • Access to your health data only occurs if you explicitly grant permission.
  • Steps are fetched locally and displayed inside the app. No step data or biological metrics are ever uploaded, synced, or shared with third parties.
  • You can disable HealthKit permissions at any time via your iOS Settings app.

3. Local Storage

Your workout history, trophy counts, weight goal logs, and onboarding parameters are stored locally on your device using **AsyncStorage**.

  • These stats are saved on your phone and are not uploaded to our cloud databases.
  • You can purge all stored logs at any time by clicking "Delete All Data" inside the Profile settings panel.

4. Policy Updates

We may modify this Privacy Policy to align with new legal requirements or SDK updates. We will notify you of any changes by updating the timestamp at the top.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding your privacy, contact us at: `privacy@aigymbro.com`.
