Core Capabilities

Engineered to elevate your form.

AI Pose Coaching

Local intelligence tracks up to 17 joints in real-time. It monitors body angles for Squats, Push-ups, Lunges, and more, offering visual feedback to help you refine your posture.

100% Offline Privacy

All math calculations occur locally on your device via MoveNet and WebGL. No video feed or biological biometric metrics ever leave your phone or get stored on remote servers.

Workout Circuit Loops

Choose from preset templates like "Cardio Blast" or "Steel Legs" with smart timers, or build your own custom circuit by checking exercises together.

Interactive Muscle Map

A beautifully rendered anatomical model visualizes your training load. Muscles light up and shift opacity depending on your total sets volume saved locally.

Level Up & XP Rewards

Earn XP points for every rep. Level up and check your status on the dashboard. Save and share premium summary templates containing workout analytics.

Streak Notifications

Set a custom hour slot during onboarding to receive localized notifications. Lock streak milestones and never miss a workout day.
